Vedda community
Field note · People

The Vedda, Sri Lanka's original forest dwellers

The Vedda are among the oldest indigenous communities in Asia, living in Sri Lanka's forests for thousands of years and passing their knowledge down by word of mouth rather than in writing.

Clad in traditional attire and often carrying a ceremonial axe, they still practise ancestral skills like archery and harvesting honey from wild hives, skills that have sustained the community for millennia.

Meeting them is not a show put on for visitors. It is a rare look at a relationship with the land that most of the modern world has forgotten.

Cinnamon peeler
CultureCeylon cinnamon

Kurundu Lihanna, the cinnamon peeler

A cinnamon farmer practising the traditional art of peeling Ceylon cinnamon, seated outside her home.

With specialised tools she scrapes away the outer bark and lifts the fragrant inner bark from each twig, a meticulous craft passed down through generations.
