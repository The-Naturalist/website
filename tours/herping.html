Day by day

Ten days and nights in Sri Lanka's richest reptile and amphibian habitats, from Wilpattu to the Knuckles range and the rainforests of Sinharaja and Runakanda.

    Day 1: Arrival→Negombo→Waikkal→Wilpattu

    Day 2: Wilpattu
  3. DAY03

  4. DAY04
    Day 4: Pidurangala→Knuckles
  5. DAY05
    Day 5: Knuckles
  6. DAY06
    Day 6: Horton Plains→Nuwara Eliya
  7. DAY07
    Day 7: Nuwara Eliya→Sinharaja
  8. DAY08
    Day 8: Sinharaja birding→Runakanda
  9. DAY09
    Day 9: Runakanda (full day)
  10. DAY10
    Day 10: Runakanda→Departure
