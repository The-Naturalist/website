Itinerary

Day by day

Eight days through the Cultural Triangle, the hill capital of Kandy, cave temples and rock carvings, and the colonial south coast.

  1. DAY01
    Day 1: Airport→Pinnawala→Dambulla→Sigiriya
  2. DAY02
    Day 2: Habarana→Polonnaruwa→Sigiriya
  3. DAY03
    Day 3: Sigiriya→Matale→Kandy
  4. DAY04
    Day 4: Kandy→Peradeniya→Nuwara Eliya
  5. DAY05
    Day 5: Nuwara Eliya→Ella→Buduruwagala→Tissamaharama
  6. DAY06
    Day 6: Tissamaharama→Mulkirigala→Koggala→Galle→Ahungalla
  7. DAY07
    Day 7: Madu Ganga→Ahungalla
  8. DAY08
    Day 8: Ahungalla→Induruwa→Colombo→Departure
