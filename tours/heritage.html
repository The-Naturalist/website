Day by day

Eight days through the Cultural Triangle, the hill capital of Kandy, cave temples and rock carvings, and the colonial south coast.

  1. DAY01
    Day 1: Airport→Pinnawala→Dambulla→Sigiriya
  2. DAY02
    Day 2: Habarana→Polonnaruwa→Sigiriya
  3. DAY03
    Day 3: Sigiriya→Matale→Kandy
  4. DAY04
    Day 4: Kandy→Peradeniya→Nuwara Eliya
  5. DAY05
    Day 5: Nuwara Eliya→Ella→Buduruwagala→Tissamaharama
  6. DAY06
    Day 6: Tissamaharama→Mulkirigala→Koggala→Galle→Ahungalla

    Day 7: Madu Ganga→Ahungalla

Common questions

When is the best time to go?

Send us your dates and we will tell you honestly what to expect and how the route can be adjusted.

How big can the group be?

Tours are private for your group. For larger groups we add a second guide.

How fit do I need to be?

Mostly drives and easy walks. Sigiriya and the Mulkirigala rock temple involve many steps, and we can set the pace or skip a climb.

Can children come?

Yes. Tell us their ages when you enquire and we will plan the pace around them.

What should I wear at temples?

Shoulders and knees covered, and clothes that are easy to take shoes off with. We brief you before each temple.

How fast do you reply?

The same day, on WhatsApp or email.
