Common questions

When is the best time to go?

Send us your dates and we will tell you honestly which birds to expect, including migrants, and how the route can be adjusted.

How big can the group be?

Tours are private for your group. For larger groups we add a second field naturalist.

How fit do I need to be?

Mostly gentle walking and safari drives, with early starts. The Horton Plains walk is the longest, and we set the pace for your group.

Do I need binoculars?

Bring your own if you have them, and ask us about gear when you enquire.

Can children come?

Yes. Tell us their ages when you enquire and we will plan the pace around them.

How fast do you reply?

The same day, on WhatsApp or email.
