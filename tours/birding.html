Day by day

Ten days following Sri Lanka's birds from coastal wetlands to cloud forest, the Sinharaja rainforest and the southern lagoons.

  1. DAY01
    Day 1: Arrival→Negombo→Muthurajawela→Kitulgala
  2. DAY02
    Day 2: Kitulgala
  3. DAY03
    Day 3: Kitulgala→Dimbula→Hakgala→Nuwara Eliya
  4. DAY04
    Day 4: Horton Plains→Victoria Park→Nuwara Eliya
  5. DAY05
    Day 5: Nuwara Eliya→Belihuloya→Sinharaja
  6. DAY06
    Day 6: Sinharaja
  7. DAY07
    Day 7: Sinharaja→Udawalawe→Tissamaharama→Yala
  8. DAY08
    Day 8: Yala→Tissamaharama
  9. DAY09
    Day 9: Tissamaharama
  10. DAY10
    Day 10: Tissamaharama→Kalametiya→Galle→Negombo→Departure
