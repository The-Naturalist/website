Day by day

Ten days from the wetlands of Negombo to the plains of Yala, through hill country forests, tea estates and four national parks.

  1. DAY01
    Day 1: Arrival→Negombo→Muthurajawela→Habarana
  2. DAY02
    Day 2: Habarana→Sigiriya→Minneriya→Habarana
  3. DAY03
    Day 3: Habarana→Matale→Mahaweli River→Udawatta Kele→Kandy
  4. DAY04
    Day 4: Kandy→Ramboda→Nuwara Eliya
  5. DAY05
    Day 5: Nuwara Eliya→Horton Plains→Nuwara Eliya
  6. DAY06
    Day 6: Nuwara Eliya→Ella
  7. DAY07
    Day 7: Ella→Udawalawe
  8. DAY08
    Day 8: Udawalawe→Bundala→Tissamaharama
  9. DAY09
    Day 9: Tissamaharama→Yala→Tissamaharama
  10. DAY10
    Day 10: Tissamaharama→Kalametiya→Galle→Departure
