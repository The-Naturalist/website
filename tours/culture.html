Day by day

Seven days through temples, spice gardens, tea country and fishing villages, ending with the coastal train ride into Colombo.

  1. DAY01
    Day 1: Arrival→Negombo→Pinnawala→Dambulla
  2. DAY02
    Day 2: Dambulla→Sigiriya→Matale→Kandy
  3. DAY03
    Day 3: Kandy→Peradeniya→Nuwara Eliya
  4. DAY04
    Day 4: Nuwara Eliya→Ella→Yala
  5. DAY05
    Day 5: Yala→Koggala→Down south
  6. DAY06
    Day 6: Down south→Madu Ganga→Down south
  7. DAY07
    Day 7: Down south→Coastal train→Colombo→Departure
