Three ways to see Colombo

Prices are per person. Message us on WhatsApp for dates.

Sunrise birds and wetlands in Colombo

A private half day starting at first light. Walk the wetlands with a trained naturalist and look for birds, monitor lizards and other urban wildlife, then stop for breakfast. AC vehicle, hotel pickup.

USD 75 per person

One day in Colombo, led by a naturalist

Start with a dawn wetland walk, then a café stop, a Buddhist temple and Dutch-era Colombo, and end with sunset at Galle Face. Private, with hotel pickup in an AC vehicle.

USD 100 per person

A full day built around you: wetlands, temples and Dutch-era heritage, at your pace, with lunch wherever you like. Premium vehicle, driver and hotel pickup.

Common questions

How many people can come?

Every option starts from 1 guest, and usual trips are for 2. Travelling with a different group size, or want a different route? Talk to us and we will customise your tour.

Can you customise a tour?

Yes. Message us what you would like to see and we will plan it with you.

Where do you pick us up?

From your hotel in Colombo.

Are meals and tickets included?

No. Entry tickets and meals are not included, so you can eat where you like and choose which sites to enter.

How fast do you reply?

The same day, on WhatsApp or email.
